Peter Mason’s practice focuses on tax and related property and commercial issues. He provides advice and strategies on lawful optimisation and, if needed, assistance in dispute resolution.

He has taken cases to tribunals and courts in the UK and the Court of Justice of the EU, where he achieved wins in groundbreaking cases.

Peter has wide experience on the government, business and professional services side. From 1980 to 1990 he worked for Customs and Excise, the then tax administration for VAT, ending up in the large business and banking division in London. He then became Head of VAT for Abbey National Bank for 14 years up to 2004. As well as managing the compliance function, he successfully took important cases to the UK and EU courts, nearly all won directly or indirectly, leading to changes in the law.

Having completed a law degree in 2004, he moved to CMS Cameron McKenna, a leading City law firm, where he was Head of VAT for nine years up to 2013, working in the UK and across the CMS European network in France, Italy, Hungary and Germany. He has experience in MTIC fraud cases and has worked on outsourcing and advisory projects in the UK, Europe, the US and India. Peter moved to boutique law firm Rosetta Tax from 2013 to 2016. From 2016 he has provided legal services locally from Cuckmere Chambers based in Sussex.

Peter is also a qualified accountant and chartered tax adviser. He can accept instructions on a direct access basis and is authorised to conduct litigation. Clients appreciate his wide experience in tax administration, business and professional legal services. This mix helps deliver a unique strategic and tactical “one stop shop” approach to issues, both “in principle” and “in amount”.

Tax — Value Added Tax (VAT)

Peter’s practice covers VAT and financial services, insurance and funds; VAT and property; and all other areas of VAT, including registration, liability, lawful optimisation of input tax recovery (including partial exemption), overseas issues, fraud and compliance.

His experience includes VAT liability of transfer pricing charges and mitigation; partial exemption reviews and recoveries for banks, funds and insurance sectors; review of collective, pension and individual fund management charges; ensuring VAT efficiencies for charitable donations; reviews of education and medical charges; review of land and property transactions; review and mitigation of penalties; review of wholesale value-chain transactions in relation to MTIC risk; review of imports and exports; review of postal and delivery services; structuring within the legal framework to obtain an optimised VAT position; VAT liability of payment services; due-diligence requirements on supplier invoices and input-tax treatment; and assisting litigation funders on likelihood of success in litigation matters.

Featured cases

  • Wagon Finance Ltd v Commissioners of Customs & Excise (CCE) [1999] UKVATTR 16288: Obtained VAT exemption on hire-purchase administration fees. Win.
  • Abbey National Plc v CCE [2001] CJEU Case C-408/98: Groundbreaking case obtaining deductibility of VAT on selling costs on transfer of going concern (TOGC) — a legal fiction which creates an “in-business non-supply” for VAT purposes. It is now possible to directly attribute expenses of a TOGC to the type of activity of the asset transferred by “looking back”, not as general overhead. This indicates how complex the issue was and is. Win.
  • Abbey National Plc v CCE [2001] UKVATTR 17506: Obtained exemption for outsourced collective investment fund management in the UK. Win.
  • Abbey National v CCE [2005] EWHC 1187 (Ch): Challenge to regulations imposing straight-line apportionment for bad-debt relief for impaired hire-purchase and conditional sale finance agreements. The case was lost, but the law changed shortly afterwards. Win in the long game.
  • Abbey National v CCE [2006] CJEU Case C-168/04: Case referred to the CJEU concerning VAT liability of fund administration for collective investment funds. The legal team traced the continuing historic obligation of an estate manager to account for the proceeds or “fruits” of an estate, from Justinian in Roman law (the “negotiorum gestio”) through Viscount Stair’s Institutes of the Laws of Scotland and continuing to modern-day Conseil d’Etat case law in France, in Sogefonds SA. The CJEU held that fund administration was a specific and essential subset of fund management, and the VAT exemption was obtained for collective investment funds. Defined-contribution pension funds have also benefited considerably from this VAT exemption. Win.
  • Abbey National v CCE [2006] EWCA Civ 886: Case concerning whether a virtual assignment of property was letting or leasing of immovable property. Part win.
  • Cabvision Ltd v Revenue and Customs Commissioners [2013] UKFTT 674 (TC): Acted as counsel in a complex case concerning direct-tax-based structured-finance arrangements and whether a credit note could be issued. Taxpayer win.
  • Intelligent Managed Services Ltd v Revenue and Customs Commissioners [2015] UKUT 341: Acted as counsel in the First-tier Tribunal and appeal to the Upper Tribunal (led by QC) on whether a business transfer into a VAT group could be a TOGC. Taxpayer win.
  • Represented a barrister in an appeal against assessments and determination of personal tax liability under self-assessment; the case settled before hearing. Taxpayer win.
  • Liability of payment services to merchants: case withdrawn because of Covid-19.

Other tax practices

Stamp duty land tax (SDLT)

Peter’s practice includes SDLT liability of property transactions, including “mixed use”, lawful optimisation of SDLT and strategic planning. His experience includes mixed-use treatment on a country house sale with land and SDLT treatment of the transfer of Reading Football Club’s stadium.

Inheritance tax (IHT)

His practice includes determination of IHT liability, in principle and amount, lawful optimisation, and reviewing estates, optimising valuation and assisting with completion of returns.

Insurance Premium Tax (IPT)

His practice includes determination of IPT liability and review of what falls into non-life general insurance under Annex I Part A 1 (Sickness) and 2 (Accident), and life (Permanent Health Insurance) under Annex II class IV.

Other taxes and all-tax advice

Peter also works on other taxes, including direct taxes and capital allowances. Across all taxes he can assist with second opinions and prospects of success in litigation. In direct-tax matters, Peter identified that US option theory could be applied in cross-border direct-tax losses. He can assist with negotiations and lobbying of tax administrations, governments and supranational bodies, and with compliance and resolution of non-compliance issues, including penalties.

Commercial and property

Peter’s practice also includes advising on commercial contracts and property transfers and leases, and claims for damages.
