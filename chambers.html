About the division

Specialist focus.
Wider perspective.

Tax expertise within a broader, complementary set of chambers.

Exchequer Chambers operated as a separate, self-contained set of Chambers specialising in tax law until July 2025.

In July 2025, its members joined the larger set of 1EC Barristers at 3 King’s Bench Walk North and now operate as a division of 1EC Barristers. All members of Exchequer Chambers became either full tenants or door tenants of 1EC Barristers and form the Exchequer Chambers division.

The merger enables matters involving commercial, insolvency, property or trust points to be dealt with comprehensively, alongside specialist tax advice.

How we work

Members provide expert advisory and litigation services in all matters relating to UK taxation. Their work also extends to professional negligence claims, trust issues, rectification and mistake claims, disciplinary proceedings against tax advisers, tax appeals and judicial review claims relating to tax.

Early-stage enquiries

A particular feature of our practices is a willingness to entertain instructions on an exploratory basis. Clients are often wary of paying large fees for what proves to be a hopeless cause. We welcome enquiries and give a preliminary view at minimum expense.

The barristers who currently form the division are listed on this site with their specialities. The team intends to grow, and new members are welcome.
