Overview

Suleman has a specialist practice in Revenue law, advising and representing individuals, businesses and international clients on complex domestic and cross-border tax matters. He accepts instructions in the Tax Tribunals, High Court and appellate courts.

Tax

His practice covers direct and indirect taxation, including tax disputes, international tax, double taxation agreements, partnership taxation, VAT, employee benefits and EBTs, and tax issues arising from business and group reorganisations. He also advises non-resident and UHNW clients on their UK tax obligations.

Suleman has particular experience in matters involving the interaction between taxation and international investment. He has advised major international clients on investment treaty disputes concerning host-state taxation, including disputes arising from representations as to the level of taxation and claims concerning unpaid tax refunds. The cumulative value of some of these claims exceeds £750 million.

He also advises on tax issues arising in partnership restructuring, insolvency and other complex commercial contexts.

Selected experience

  • Advising major clients in the oil and gas sector in investment treaty arbitration arising from a host state’s representation that the total tax levied during the investment period would not exceed a specified percentage of profits.
  • Advising international clients in investment treaty disputes arising from host states’ failure to make tax refunds due for more than a decade. The cumulative value of the claims exceeds £750 million.
  • Advising partnerships on the mixed partnership rules.
  • Advising on international tax issues arising under double taxation conventions.
  • Advising on VAT disputes arising in the recruitment and other sectors.
  • Advising non-resident UHNW clients on their UK tax obligations.
  • Challenging Schedule 36 Notices.
  • Advising on employee benefits and Employee Benefit Trusts.
  • Advising on issues arising from failed film schemes.
  • Advising on the availability of agricultural and other tax reliefs.
