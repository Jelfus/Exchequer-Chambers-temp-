Advisory work, disputes and litigation — from domestic tax questions to cross-border matters and connected commercial issues.

Members of the Exchequer Chambers division provide expert advisory and litigation services in all matters relating to UK taxation.

Tax advice and planning

Our barristers advise on preventative planning and lawful tax optimisation, as well as the resolution of contentious disputes. The division’s experience spans direct and indirect taxes, employee remuneration, share schemes, trusts, inheritance tax and capital gains tax planning.

Members also advise on VAT, SDLT, stamp taxes, customs and excise duties, international tax, double taxation agreements, partnership taxation and tax issues arising in business reorganisations.

Disputes and appeals

We act in tax disputes, tax appeals and judicial review claims relating to tax, including matters before tribunals and the courts. Our work includes professional negligence claims, challenges to revenue decisions, and disciplinary proceedings against tax advisers.

Members have experience of litigation in the UK and in other common law jurisdictions, including Hong Kong and the Caribbean.

Trusts and related claims

Our work includes trust issues, rectification and mistake claims, estate and trust planning, and disputes involving charities. We can also draw on the wider expertise of 1EC Barristers when a matter engages commercial, insolvency, property or trust points.

Exploratory instructions

We understand that clients may be cautious about incurring substantial fees before knowing whether a case has merit. We welcome exploratory enquiries and are willing to provide a preliminary view at minimum expense, helping clients understand the options before deciding how to proceed.
