Exchequer Chambers

Specialist Tax
Barristers

Exchequer Chambers operated as a separate, self-contained set of Chambers specialising in tax law until July 2025. The then members of Exchequer Chambers joined the larger set of 1EC Barristers at 3 King’s Bench Walk North and now operate as a division of 1EC Barristers. All members of Exchequer Chambers became either full tenants or door tenants of 1EC Barristers and form the Exchequer Chambers division. The merger with 1EC Barristers enables matters covering commercial, insolvency, property or trust points to be dealt with comprehensively.

Members of the Exchequer Chambers division provide expert advisory and litigation services in all matters relating to UK taxation. These services extend to professional negligence claims, trust issues, rectification and mistake claims, disciplinary proceedings against tax advisers as well as tax appeals and judicial review claims relating to tax. A particular feature of our practices is that we are willing to entertain instructions on an exploratory basis. Very often clients are wary of paying large fees for what proves to be a hopeless cause. Our policy is to welcome enquiries and give a preliminary view at minimum expense.

The barristers who currently form our division are set out below with their specialities. Our intention is to increase the team. New members are welcome.

Some members have separate specialist practices. A call to Ian Hogg will direct you to the right contact: 020 3150 0001.
